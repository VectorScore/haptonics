# Haptonics editing preferences

- Unless the user explicitly requests a change to raVe, visual tuning requests apply only to the custom effects (line, particles, spikes).
- Keep raVe's original drawing equations, audio filters/defaults, radii and color feedback unchanged. Do not add custom transient scaling, glow or custom color overrides to raVe. Pets may now be composited above the unchanged raVe renderer, as explicitly requested. Its effect controls are intentionally locked; independent pet selection remains available.
- Center pets in custom effects, use restrained outline designs (cat: two whiskers per side; bear: a small nose, as explicitly requested), and preserve synchronized phone/watch rendering. Prefer restrained motion over strong shaking or flashes.

- The user’s >=100 Hz requirement applies only to the built-in metronome motor carrier. Do not impose a 100 Hz minimum, high-pass cutoff, or frequency clamp on incoming audio or audio-driven effects. Keep carrier frequency separate from musical BPM and visual frame rate.

- Place app dialogs inside the phone viewport, with title, content, then close. Keep normal punctuation in explanation paragraphs; omit terminal full stops only in captions/subtitles.
